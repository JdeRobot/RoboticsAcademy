# Square with Tello Drone Exercise

## Exercise Setup

### Required Hardware
- Tello drone
- Compatible smartphone or tablet
- Tello app installed on the smartphone or tablet

### Software Installation
1. Install the Tello app on your smartphone or tablet from the App Store or Google Play Store.
2. Ensure your Tello drone is fully charged.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Connect to the Tello Drone
1. Turn on your Tello drone by pressing the power button.
2. Open the Wi-Fi settings on your smartphone or tablet and connect to the Tello drone's Wi-Fi network.

### Step 2: Open the Tello App
1. Launch the Tello app on your smartphone or tablet.
2. Ensure the app is connected to the Tello drone.

### Step 3: Take Off
1. In the Tello app, tap the "Take Off" button to make the drone hover in the air.

### Step 4: Fly in a Square Pattern
1. Use the on-screen controls in the Tello app to fly the drone in a square pattern.
2. Move the drone forward for a few seconds, then turn 90 degrees to the right.
3. Repeat this process three more times to complete the square pattern.

### Step 5: Land the Drone
1. Once the square pattern is complete, tap the "Land" button in the Tello app to safely land the drone.

## Troubleshooting Tips and Common Issues

### Issue: Drone Not Connecting to Wi-Fi
- Ensure the drone is powered on.
- Check if the Wi-Fi network is visible in your smartphone or tablet's Wi-Fi settings.
- Restart the drone and try connecting again.

### Issue: Drone Not Taking Off
- Ensure the drone's battery is fully charged.
- Check if the drone is connected to the Tello app.
- Restart the Tello app and try taking off again.

### Issue: Drone Not Responding to Controls
- Ensure the drone is within the Wi-Fi range of your smartphone or tablet.
- Check if there are any obstacles or interference affecting the drone's signal.
- Restart the Tello app and try controlling the drone again.
